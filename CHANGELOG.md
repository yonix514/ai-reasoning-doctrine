# Changelog

## [1.0.0] — 2026-10-12

First release.

### Added

- `doctrine/14-principes.md` — fourteen reasoning principles, each born from a
  measured mistake, with the measurement that produced it.
- `doctrine/les-14-lunettes.md` — fourteen expert "lenses" and the **Council of
  14** method: pick five lenses, force them to disagree, arbitrate, decide.
- `doctrine/8-types-erreurs.md` — a taxonomy of eight error types (construction,
  absence, nature, instrument, multiplicity, temporality, order, declaration),
  plus the mandatory-contradictor protocol.
- `doctrine/explorer-une-api.md` — how to explore an undocumented API by
  reflection instead of guessing at names and units.
- English translations of the principles and the error taxonomy.

### Changed

- **Private case studies were removed.** The doctrine grew out of a private
  working system and cited its projects, figures and internal file paths. All of
  that has been removed or generalised before publication. No figure was
  replaced with an invented one; where a case study was private, it is marked as
  removed.
- The video-editing, Vegas Pro scripting and ffmpeg material **is kept**: that is
  the author's own craft, and it is where most of these principles were learned.
